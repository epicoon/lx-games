# Outposts of the Void — rules

A game for 2-4 players. Each player builds their own outpost, filling
a hexagonal building zone with tokens of different types. Whoever
scores the most points by the end of the game wins.

The game runs **5 phases of 5 rounds each**. At the start of every
round, each player's 2 dice are rolled automatically, along with a
shared die that determines which of the six numbered storages receives
that round's mineral. Players then take their turns in turn order (the
sequence track — further right/higher goes earlier).

## How you score

Points come from two places: continuously through the game, and at
the very end.

During the game you score right away by closing clusters, placing
Drone tokens, splitting minerals, placing Telescope modules.

At the end of the game you additionally score for your remaining Data
Storage units (1 point per two units) and energy credits (1 point per
unit), for unsplit minerals (1 point per mineral), points for any
medals you hold, and roughly half of the Technologies, which give
points only at this point.

## Your turn

Two actions per turn, one per own die:

- **Take a token from the shared board.** Take any token from the
  numbered storage matching your die's value and put it into your
  own storage (not into your building zone).
- **Add a token to your building zone.** Take a token from your own
  storage and place it into a cell whose color matches the token and
  whose number matches the die. A new token must be adjacent to an
  already-placed one; the exception is before the game starts, when
  you place your command station token anywhere suitable in your
  building zone. A cluster of Modules can't contain more than one of
  the same Module (unless a Technology lets you ignore this rule).
- **Exchange a die for Data Storage.** Instead of using a die for an
  action, you can exchange it for 2 Data Storage units (4 with a
  certain Technology).

Besides the two die actions, once per turn, at any point, you may also
**buy one token** from the central black storage for 2 energy credits.

A **Data Storage** unit can be spent at any time on your turn to
change any die's value by ±1 (6+1 gives 1, 1-1 gives 6).

## Token types

Placing a token into your building zone triggers its effect
immediately, unless noted otherwise:

- **Mining Probe** — lets you take minerals from any numbered storage
  onto your own board, and moves your turn-order token one step
  forward.
- **Command Station** — grants one extra action, as if you had a
  spare die showing whatever value you need.
- **Solar Panel** — no immediate effect; at the end of **each phase**
  every Solar Panel you have gives 1 energy credit.
- **Drone** (4 kinds: Service, Scout, Cargo, Support) — scores points
  immediately; if the same kind of drone is already in the same
  cluster, points are recalculated for every drone of that kind in
  the cluster.
- **Technology** (26 unique tiles) — can change a rule for you, or
  provide extra bonuses to your actions. About half give points that
  only score at the end of the game. Hover over a Technology token
  in-game for its exact effect.
- **Module** (8 kinds, always an immediate effect):
  - *Matter Splitter* — split minerals;
  - *Assembly Module* — take any Module token from any storage;
  - *Research Laboratory* — take a Solar Panel, Command Station, or
    Technology token from any storage;
  - *Drone Manufacturing Plant* — take a Mining Probe or Drone token
    from any storage;
  - *Supercomputer* — gain 4 Data Storage units;
  - *Tokamak* — gain 2 energy credits;
  - *Roboport* — immediately place a token already sitting in your
    own storage into your building zone;
  - *Telescope* — +4 victory points right away.

## Minerals

Minerals arrive in the numbered storages according to the shared
die's roll. Adding a Mining Probe to your building zone lets you take
minerals from any storage onto your own board.

Minerals on your own board can be **split** (via a die action, or the
Matter Splitter module): all minerals of one type move to your split
pile and score points right away for every mineral in the group,
based on the number of players:

| Players | Points per split mineral |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | 2 |
| 3 | 3 |
| 4 | 4 |

Splitting a large group at once scores points for every mineral in it.
Splitting also gives 1 energy credit right away - once for the whole
group regardless of its size (2 with a certain Technology).

Some Technologies add a Data Storage unit when you split, and others
later score extra points for everything sitting in your split pile at
the end of the game.

## Filling a cluster

When the last token closes a cluster completely (every adjacent cell
of one color is filled), it scores points right away based on the
cluster's size:

| Cluster size (cells) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Points | 1 | 3 | 6 | 10 | 15 | 21 | 28 | 36 |

Plus a bonus for the current phase:

| Phase | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bonus | 10 | 8 | 6 | 4 | 2 |

The first player to fill in **every** cell of one color in their
building zone takes that color's gold medal from the shared board.
The second player to do the same for that color takes the silver
medal. Each medal scores points at the end of the game, based on the
number of players:

| Players | Gold medal | Silver medal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 5 | 2 |
| 3 | 6 | 3 |
| 4 | 7 | 4 |

## End of game

The game ends after the fifth round of the fifth phase, followed by
final scoring: remaining Data Storage units and energy credits,
unsplit minerals, points for any medals you hold, and any Technology
that only scores at game end. Whoever has the most points wins.

For a specific token's exact effect, hover over it in-game - a
tooltip explains it.
